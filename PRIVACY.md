# Privacy Policy

**mcp-virtualbox** runs entirely on your local machine. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or telemetry.

- All VM operations are performed locally via VirtualBox on your computer
- No data is sent to any external server
- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No accounts or registration required

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/chryaner/mcp-virtualbox/issues.
